# Earth Elemental Companion

This companion begins as a daisy rooted in living earth. With patient care, it grows into a tiny elemental, then into larger forms until it becomes a full earth elemental. It is powerful, loyal, slow to recover, and never meant to be treated as a disposable tank.

## Growth Cycle

The elemental advances one stage after each full week of nurturing. Nurturing requires daily attention, access to soil or stone, and at least a little water and sunlight. The DM may pause the growth cycle if the elemental is neglected, kept from earth for too long, or exposed to hostile magic.

| Stage | Time Nurtured | Form | Size | Hit Points | Stone Dice | Dice Regained on Long Rest |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| 0 | Starting form | Daisy | Tiny plant | 1 | None | 0 |
| 1 | 1 week | Pebbleling | Tiny elemental | 9 | 2d4 | 1 |
| 2 | 2 weeks | Clodling | Small elemental | 22 | 4d6 | 2 |
| 3 | 3 weeks | Stonekin | Medium elemental | 45 | 6d8 | 2 |
| 4 | 4 weeks | Earth Elemental | Large elemental | 126 | 12d10 | 3 |

## Companion Rules

**Bonded Companion.** The elemental is bonded to the creature that nurtures it. It is friendly to that creature and its allies unless mistreated. Once it reaches Stage 1, it can speak Common and Terran.

**No Magical Healing.** The elemental cannot regain Hit Points from healing potions, healing spells, or other magical healing unless the DM specifically rules that an effect restores earth, stone, or elemental matter.

**Stone Dice.** The elemental has a fixed pool of Hit Dice called Stone Dice, shown in the Growth Cycle table. During a Short Rest or Long Rest, it can spend Stone Dice to regain Hit Points as normal. When it finishes a Long Rest while touching natural earth or stone, it regains only the number of spent Stone Dice listed in the Growth Cycle table. It does not automatically return to full Hit Points after a Long Rest.

**Rooted Recovery.** If the elemental spends an entire Long Rest planted, buried, or resting motionless against natural earth or stone, it can spend Stone Dice at the end of the rest. If it cannot touch earth or stone for the rest, it regains no Stone Dice.

**Daisy Rebirth.** If the elemental is reduced to 0 Hit Points, its body collapses into soil and stone. A daisy immediately sprouts in its space or in the nearest unoccupied patch of earth. The elemental returns to Stage 0 and must grow again from the beginning. Any spent Stone Dice are reset to the Daisy stage.

## Action Economy

The elemental acts on the bonded creature's initiative, immediately after that creature's turn.

**No Command.** If the bonded creature gives no command, the elemental takes the Dodge action and moves only to protect itself, stay near the bonded creature, or avoid obvious danger.

**Bonus Action Command.** The bonded creature can use a Bonus Action to command the elemental. The command allows the elemental to move and take one action from its stat block.

**Full Action Command.** At Stage 4, the bonded creature must use its own action if it wants the elemental to use Multiattack. If commanded with only a Bonus Action, the Stage 4 elemental can make one Slam attack instead.

**Independent Instinct.** The elemental may act without a command if the bonded creature is incapacitated, unconscious, or unable to communicate. In that case, it protects the bonded creature, defends itself, or retreats with the bonded creature if possible.

**Shared Limits.** The elemental cannot attune to magic items, drink potions, activate magic items, Help with tasks requiring speech or hands, or take actions that require complex reasoning unless the DM allows it.

## Optional Rule: Growth Rings

Do not make the elemental stronger every time it falls to 0 Hit Points. That can encourage reckless play or intentional sacrifice.

Instead, the DM may award a Growth Ring when the elemental is destroyed during a meaningful act of protection, sacrifice, or story importance. A Growth Ring applies only once the elemental grows back to at least Stage 1.

- Maximum Growth Rings: 3.
- Each Growth Ring increases the elemental's maximum Hit Points by 1 per Stone Die.
- Growth Rings are awarded by the DM, not triggered automatically.
- A Growth Ring may leave a visible mark, such as crystal veins, scorched stone, moss, clay, or glassy cracks.

Random vulnerabilities are not recommended as a default rule. They are easy to forget and can become either a punishment or an exploit. If the DM wants a scar from a major death, use a visible story mark instead.

## Stage 0: Daisy

*Tiny Plant, Unaligned*

**Armor Class** 10  
**Hit Points** 1  
**Speed** 0 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (-5) | 1 (-5) | 10 (+0) | 3 (-4) | 10 (+0) | 6 (-2) |

**Damage Vulnerabilities** Fire  
**Damage Immunities** Poison  
**Condition Immunities** Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned, Prone  
**Senses** Tremorsense 5 ft. while rooted in earth, Passive Perception 10  
**Languages** Understands Common and Terran but cannot speak

### Traits

**Dormant Elemental.** The daisy is the resting seed of the elemental. It cannot take actions or reactions.

**Nurtured Growth.** After 1 full week of daily care, the daisy becomes a Pebbleling.

### Traits

**Earth Sense.** The pebbleling knows the direction of its bonded creature while both are touching the same ground and within 1 mile of each other.

**Slam.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +3, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d4 + 1) Bludgeoning damage.
