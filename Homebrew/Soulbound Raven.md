# Soulbound Raven

*Tiny Beast, Unaligned*

Use this raven as a strange bonded companion, ominous familiar, or cursed scouting tool. It is not a normal animal once bonded; its soul is partially anchored to the creature it bonds with.

## Stat Block

**Armor Class** 13  
**Hit Points** 10  
**Speed** 10 ft., Fly 50 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 (-4) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 3 (-4) | 14 (+2) | 6 (-2) |

**Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +5  
**Damage Immunities** Damage from spells, magic items, and other magical effects  
**Condition Immunities** Any condition imposed by a spell, magic item, or other magical effect  
**Senses** Passive Perception 14  
**Languages** Understands the languages of its bonded creature but cannot speak  
**Challenge** 0

### Traits

**Keen Sight.** The raven has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

**Magic Null.** The raven is immune to spells, magic items, and other magical effects unless this stat block says otherwise. It automatically succeeds on saving throws against magical effects, takes no damage from magical sources, and cannot be healed by healing spells, healing potions, or other magical healing.

**Soul Bond.** A willing raven can spend 1 uninterrupted hour bonding with a creature it chooses, remaining within 5 feet of that creature for the duration. The raven decides whether the bond forms when the hour is complete; if it refuses, no bond forms. A creature can be bonded to only one Soulbound Raven at a time, and the raven can be bonded to only one creature at a time.

While bonded, the bonded creature and the raven can communicate telepathically while within 1 mile of each other. This communication does not require a shared language.

**Borrowed Senses.** Five times per Long Rest, the bonded creature can take a Magic action to see and hear through the raven's senses for up to 10 minutes, provided the raven is within 1 mile. During this time, the bonded creature is Blinded and Deafened with regard to its own senses. The bonded creature can end this effect early with no action required.

**Raven's Conduit.** Once per Long Rest, when the bonded creature casts a spell or uses an ability, it can treat the raven's space as its own for determining the spell or ability's point of origin, range, and line of sight. The bonded creature must still provide any required components, costs, actions, uses, or other requirements. If the spell or ability would directly target or affect the raven, that part of the effect fails because of Magic Null.

### Actions

**Beak.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 1 Piercing damage.

## Bond Rules

### Voluntary Severing

The bonded creature can willingly end the soul bond by spending 10 uninterrupted minutes severing the connection while the raven is within 5 feet. The bond does not end until the 10 minutes are complete.

If the bonded creature willingly severs the bond this way, it suffers no penalty.

### Forced Break

The soul bond breaks immediately if any of the following occur:

- The bonded creature is reduced to 0 Hit Points.
- The raven is reduced to 0 Hit Points.
- The bonded creature or the raven dies.
- The bonded creature and the raven are ever more than 1 mile apart.

When the bond breaks this way, the bonded creature suffers the following effects until it finishes a Long Rest or bonds with the raven again:

- Its Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores become 4.
- It has Disadvantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws.
- It gains 1 level of Exhaustion.
