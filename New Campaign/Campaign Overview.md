# New Campaign Overview

This is the working outline for a homebrew campaign beginning at level 1. Each chapter covers one character level. Details can stay flexible until the players and their characters help shape the world.

## Campaign Premise

A powerful company controls the rare magical crystals found in a small region east of a dangerous mountain range. The crystals hold tremendous energy and can power machines, devices, and airships.

The company's founder was a kind and talented inventor. His work began a magical industrial revolution, connecting isolated regions and making travel and trade faster and safer. After his death, his son inherited the company and transformed it into a monopoly. He bought back privately owned airships and devices, replaced them with weaker products, and forced the public to depend upon company-controlled transportation and shipping.

A war was fought over control of the crystals, and the company won. Crystals are now scarce among the public, while travel and trade have become prohibitively expensive. The starting region has become economically dependent on the company, leaving employment there as one of the few ways to survive.

The company is secretly transporting more than goods. It traffics people, deals in dangerous creatures, and funds wars that increase dependence on its services. A resistance based largely west of the mountains is working to break the monopoly and restore public access to the crystals.

## The World

### The Starting Region

A small alcove east of the mountains where the crystal mines were discovered. The company watches the region closely, dominates its economy, and suppresses resistance activity.

### The Dangerous Mountains

A hazardous mountain range separating the western lands from the starting region and the islands to the east. Before airships, travelers crossed broken and dangerous terrain where distance, weather, and hostile creatures made trade slow and costly.

### The Sky City

High above the eternal mist and clouds, a tall mountain peak has been transformed into a protected paradise for the wealthy. It hosts balls, celebrations, and other extravagant events. It can only be reached through the sky, keeping its residents safely removed from the people affected by the company's rule.

### The Outer Regions

- The western region, where the resistance has its strongest presence
- The broken islands, remnants of the old and dangerous trade route
- A volcanic island to the northeast
- An undeveloped island or region to the southeast

## Important People and Factions

### The Company

The dominant producer, shipper, and transporter of crystal-powered technology. Publicly, it provides jobs and connects the world. Secretly, it maintains its control through exploitation, trafficking, political influence, and war.

### The Current Owner

The founder's son, who turned the company into a monopoly. He is responsible for the company's abuses, but may also be working with a greater villain. One possibility is a devil who amplifies his greed and ambition without removing his responsibility for his choices.

### The Founder

The original creator of the company and its crystal-powered technology. He was remembered as kind and generous, although the complete truth of the company's early history is still open for development.

### The Old Engineer

A former engineer and independent craftsman who once worked with crystal-powered machines. He lost most of his business when the company seized control of production. His workshop connects to a large warehouse containing an old airship that he has been quietly restoring. He could be related to one of the player characters.

### The Resistance

A movement trying to end the company's monopoly and return access to crystal technology to the wider world. Most of its strength is west of the mountains because the starting region is heavily monitored.

## The Ancient Mine Key

The Ancient Mine Key is a unique physical artifact connected to stories of a valuable crystal mine hidden somewhere deep within the dangerous mountains. According to those stories, the mine lies behind an ancient sealed vault that cannot be opened without the Key.

The mine's true location has been lost. Its map was divided into pieces that are now scattered across different regions of the world. The pieces must be recovered and assembled before anyone can find the vault. Knowledge of the Key cannot replace the physical artifact, and information about one map piece cannot reveal the full route.

If the resistance can secure the Key and recover the map, it may gain access to a crystal supply outside the company's control and threaten the foundation of the monopoly.

## How Airships Work

Airships combine a ship-like hull with several systems that control lift, direction, and speed.

### Lift Balloon

A large rigid or semi-rigid balloon sits above the hull. Several internal cells hold hydrogen, whose low density supports most of the ship's weight. The cells are separated so that damage to one does not immediately bring down the entire vessel. Hydrogen provides strong lift and suits the world's industrial technology, but its flammability makes fire a serious danger aboard any airship.

### Lift Canisters

Reinforced canisters store a smaller reserve of compressed hydrogen. Crystal-powered pumps move this gas between the canisters and flexible trim cells inside the balloon. Expanding the trim cells increases lift, while compressing their gas back into the canisters reduces lift. This system is used for gradual altitude adjustments rather than storing all the ship's lifting gas.

The ship is designed to be close to neutrally buoyant under normal load. Cargo weight and ballast affect that balance. Crew members can drop ballast for an emergency ascent or vent hydrogen for an emergency descent, but either choice consumes a limited resource.

### Rear Propellers

Propellers mounted at the back of the ship provide forward thrust. A crystal-powered engine drives them through mechanical shafts and gears. Their speed determines how quickly the vessel moves through the air.

### Steering Wings

Wing-like drag panels sit on both sides near the rear of the ship. Opening one creates drag on that side, turning the ship toward the open panel in the same way that placing an oar into the water helps turn a canoe. Opening both panels creates drag evenly and acts as the ship's brake. These panels become more effective as the ship moves faster and have little effect while it is hovering.

### Basic Controls

- Pump reserve hydrogen into the trim cells to rise gradually
- Compress hydrogen back into the canisters to descend gradually
- Increase propeller speed to accelerate
- Reduce propeller speed to coast
- Open the left wing to turn left
- Open the right wing to turn right
- Open both wings to slow or stop
- Drop ballast for an emergency ascent
- Vent hydrogen for an emergency descent

## The Party's Airship

During the first five levels, the party helps the old engineer find the remaining parts needed to restore his airship, including a suitable power crystal. Once operational, the ship allows the party to escape the starting region and reach the wider world.

The airship becomes the party's first bastion. Initially, it has only two usable rooms, encouraging the party to repair, improve, and personalize it as the campaign continues.

### Missing Restoration Components

1. **Lift canisters and compressor:** Reinforced hydrogen canisters and the pump needed to control the ship's trim cells.
2. **Propeller drive gearbox:** The precision gears and drive assembly that transfer power from the engine to the rear propellers.
3. **Steering-wing assembly:** The synchronized controls, cables, and actuators used to open and close the two rear drag panels.
4. **Power crystal:** A high-output crystal capable of powering the restored ship's engine and lift pumps.

# Campaign Chapters

## Chapter 1: Welcome to the Company

**Character level:** 1  
**Primary purpose:** Introduce the company, airships, crystal technology, the Ancient Mine Key, and the campaign's central moral conflict.

The party is barely making ends meet and applies for work with the company, one of the only reliable employers in the region. Their interview includes a guided tour of the main plant:

1. Shipping and logistics
2. Packing and storage
3. The sky deck, where airships attach to load and unload cargo

During the tour, powerful beasts break free from a newly arrived ship. The party must help contain the emergency. During the chaos, they discover restrained people hidden aboard the ship, revealing that the company is transporting people against their will.

During the same incident, the party comes into possession of the Ancient Mine Key. It may be entrusted to them by a captured courier or found among belongings confiscated from the prisoners. They do not initially understand what it is or why the company considers it so dangerous.

Company guards or supervisors order the party to focus on protecting valuable property and ignore the prisoners. How the characters respond can determine who in the company trusts them, who becomes suspicious of them, and whether any prisoners or witnesses remember their actions.

The incident ends the interview. Whether the party openly defies the guards, sees something the company needs concealed, or is made into a convenient scapegoat, the company refuses to employ them. They leave without jobs, carrying an artifact the company wants recovered and dangerous knowledge that company officials want contained.

The old engineer witnesses the party fight during the escape. Afterward, he overhears company officials discussing how to silence them. He follows or searches for the party, reaching them when company agents corner them in an alley or confront them at their inn, rented rooms, or home. He helps them get away and offers them a safe place to hide.

**End of chapter:** The party survives the incident, loses its chance at company employment, and accepts the old engineer's offer of shelter.

## Chapter 2: The Old Engineer

**Character level:** 2  
**Primary purpose:** Give the party a refuge, reveal the western resistance, and make restoring the airship the party's path toward fighting back.

The old engineer hides the party in his struggling workshop and offers them paid work while they remain out of the company's reach. As they help him with odd jobs, they learn about his history with crystal-powered machines, the damage caused by the company's monopoly, and the airship hidden in his adjoining warehouse.

After hearing what the party witnessed and examining the artifact they recovered, the engineer identifies it as the Ancient Mine Key. He explains that the resistance beyond the mountains has been searching for the scattered pieces of a lost map to the mine. If the resistance received the Key and completed the map, it might gain access to crystals outside the company's control and begin breaking the monopoly.

The party cannot trust company-controlled transportation to carry the artifact. The hidden airship offers a way to deliver it, but it cannot yet fly. The engineer needs four difficult-to-obtain systems: its lift canisters and compressor, propeller drive gearbox, steering-wing assembly, and a suitable power crystal. Restoring the ship becomes the party's plan for reaching the western resistance.

## Chapter 3: First Contact

**Character level:** 3  
**Primary purpose:** Establish the party's first direct contact with the resistance without fully resolving whether it can be trusted.

While searching for the steering-wing assembly, the party finds a local resistance contact or a way to exchange messages with the western movement. They learn that operating openly in the starting region is extremely dangerous and that the resistance cannot safely extract them without the airship.

The company's hidden activities should become clearer during this chapter. Its trafficking and support for distant wars are not isolated crimes, but parts of a larger system that protects its monopoly.

**Details to develop:**

- Where the steering-wing assembly is located
- The party's first resistance contact
- What the resistance asks the party to risk
- A reason the party might distrust part of the resistance

**End of chapter:** The party gains the steering-wing assembly and a possible connection to the western resistance.

## Chapter 4: The Propeller Drive

**Character level:** 4  
**Primary purpose:** Restore the ship's propulsion and force the party into direct conflict with the company.

The airship requires a precision drive gearbox to transfer power from its engine to the rear propellers. This old or restricted machinery may be held in a company warehouse, installed in a grounded vessel, or possessed by someone unwilling to surrender it.

The party's actions attract serious company attention. The workshop and anyone connected to it are no longer safe.

**Details to develop:**

- Where the propeller drive gearbox is located
- Why it cannot simply be purchased or recreated
- Who else wants or protects it
- The cost or consequence of taking it

**End of chapter:** The party obtains the propeller drive gearbox, but the company discovers enough of their plans to begin closing in.

## Chapter 5: The Power Crystal

**Character level:** 5  
**Primary purpose:** Secure the ship's final power source, launch the airship, and open the wider campaign.

The airship requires a powerful crystal that private citizens are forbidden or unable to obtain. The party must acquire one from a company reserve, guarded shipment, damaged vessel, or another source within the starting region. The hidden ancient mine will remain inaccessible until much later in the campaign.

Once the party obtains the crystal, company forces move against the workshop. The party must install the final component, finish the repairs, and launch the ship under pressure. The old engineer stays behind to hold off the attackers and give the airship enough time to escape.

As the restored airship pulls away, the party watches the engineer's workshop and warehouse burn. They have every reason to believe that he died in the fire. With only two functional rooms, the ship is useful but visibly incomplete, and it becomes the party's mobile bastion as they escape the starting region and fly above the dangerous mountains toward the western lands.

**GM truth:** The engineer survives the fire and is captured by the company. His survival remains secret until the company reveals that it is holding him and attempts to use his life as leverage against the party.

**Details to develop:**

- How the party learns about and reaches it
- Who else wants it
- The cost or consequence of taking it
- The final repair or complication during installation
- Who attacks or betrays the party
- How the company later proves that the engineer survived
- What the company demands in exchange for his life
- What they see while crossing above the eternal mist

**End of chapter:** The level 5 party escapes company control, believes the old engineer died to save them, and gains access to the wider world.

# Later Chapters

Each later chapter will continue to cover one level. Their exact order and level range can be decided after the first arc and player character stories are clearer. Recovering the scattered pieces of the ancient mine map gives the party a reason to visit the world's major regions before attempting the dangerous mountain expedition.

## Western Region Arc

The party spends several levels working with, against, or between resistance factions in the western lands. The resistance can share its research into the ancient mine, including a map piece or the first lead toward one. This arc can reveal how the company's actions affect distant lands and whether simply destroying the monopoly would actually restore balance.

## Broken Islands Arc

The airship takes the party among the broken islands along the old trade route in search of another map piece. This region can explore what existed before air travel and what the company prefers travelers never find.

## Volcanic Island Arc

The party travels to the volcanic island northeast of the mountains in search of another map piece. Its relationship to the crystals, the company, and the wider conflict remains to be developed.

## Southeastern Region Arc

The southeastern island or region holds another part of the map and remains open for a distinct culture, conflict, and revelation tied to the larger campaign.

## The Ancient Mine Arc

Once the map is complete, the party can finally determine the vault's true location. At a higher level, they must undertake the dangerous journey into the mountains, reach the sealed entrance, and use the Ancient Mine Key. The mine can reveal the origin of the crystals, why the vault was sealed, and whether opening it is actually safe for the world.

## Final Arc: The City Above the Clouds

The party eventually returns to the wealthy sky city for the final push against the company owner and the greater villain working with him. The goal is not merely to remove one person, but to decide how the crystals, airships, and other resources can be returned to the world without creating another monopoly or destructive power struggle.
