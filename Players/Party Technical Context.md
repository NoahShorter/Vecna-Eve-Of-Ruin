## Chapter 6 Implications

This party is very strong at bypassing, scouting, and controlling the chapter's set pieces.

- `Sunburst`: Rhivi has it. The chapter specifically says `Sunburst` suppresses solid moonlight for 10 minutes, so Rhivi can meaningfully disrupt upper-level barriers, moonbridges, and the moondisk if they choose to spend the 8th-level slot.
- Stealth/infiltration: Jiguard is an elite infiltrator, Varyn has stealth support gear, Zagdreth has Boots of Elvenkind, and Rhivi has `Pass without Trace`. If the party chooses a heist approach, let it work, but decide alarm logic before play.
- Puzzle discovery: Zagdreth's passive Investigation is 25 and Jiguard's is 23. Hidden clues like the V20 mirror should not be a long-term blocker if they search.
- Magical bypass: Zagdreth has `Passwall`, `Dimension Door`, `Teleport`, `Plane Shift`, `Fly`, `Wall of Force`, and `Forcecage`. Solid moonlight blocks teleportation through it, but normal vault walls, locked routes, verticality, and many encounters are vulnerable to magical routing.
- Counter-magic: Sadie, Varyn, and Zagdreth have `Counterspell`; Rhivi, Sadie, and Zagdreth have `Dispel Magic`. Teremini should expect magical opposition.
- Social pressure: Sadie can carry negotiation and deception scenes at +15. Gazaia, Casivus, captured enemies, and even Lord Soth's scrying sphere can become social scenes if you let them.
- Healing/recovery: Rhivi and Sadie both have major healing, and Jiguard has emergency cleric healing. A pure attrition dungeon will be hard to make threatening unless alarms, objectives, and time pressure matter.
- Dragon/finale control: `Forcecage`, `Wall of Force`, `Banishment`, `Polymorph`, `Globe of Invulnerability`, and multiple counterspells can heavily affect Teremini and Orinix. Run the finale as a chaotic objective scene, not only a stand-still slugfest.

## Party Strengths

- Investigation and trap work: excellent.
- Stealth and infiltration: excellent, especially with `Pass without Trace`.
- Social manipulation: excellent through Sadie.
- Arcane problem solving: excellent through Zagdreth, plus Varyn and Sadie.
- Counterspell/dispel coverage: excellent.
- Healing: strong.
- Radiant/anti-undead tools: strong.
- Mobility and escape: very strong.

## Party Watchouts

- Jiguard has low max HP for this tier at 71.
- Rhivi's AC is low at 13, though druid tools may compensate.
- Several characters rely on concentration; Teremini, Akaazi, and Orinix should pressure concentration rather than only dealing raw damage.
- If the party gets unrestricted long rests, they can solve most problems with spell slots. Use the 12-hour Warden protection, Teremini's ritual progress, alarms, and prisoner danger as soft clocks.
- The party can probably bypass a large portion of Three Moons Vault. Prepare the important information as mobile clues rather than locking it to one room.

## DM Prep Adjustments For This Party

- Give the mirror puzzle clear physicality. These players have the tools to solve it; the challenge should be choosing under pressure, not guessing what the DM wants.
- Prepare consequences for a very successful stealth route: skipped rooms should still leave enough clues through Valendar, Casivus, and the logbook.
- Let `Sunburst` matter. If Rhivi uses it on solid moonlight, that is a high-level spell spent on exactly the right problem.
- Avoid relying on one locked door, one hidden object, or one wall as a required bottleneck.
- Use layered objectives in combat: save wardens, stop horns, protect Riffel, remove mirrors, reach crystals, claim the rod, escape Orinix.
- Give Teremini a plan for counterspells, line of sight, minion screens, and retreat/repositioning. A lone archmage in open space is vulnerable to this party.
- Consider giving Orinix arrival beats that create terrain collapse, forced movement, or competing priorities rather than only another high-HP target.
